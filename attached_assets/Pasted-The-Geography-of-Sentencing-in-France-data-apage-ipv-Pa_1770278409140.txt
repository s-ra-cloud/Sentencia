The Geography of Sentencing in France data apage (ipv)

Page Layout and Structure
The page is organized into three main sections.
On the left-hand side, a contextual presentation panel introduces the analytical scope of the page. This section explains that the map represents judicial outcomes, not crime prevalence or reported offences. It outlines the methodological principles underlying the visualization, including the use of administrative sentencing data, aggregation at the departmental level, and the main interpretative limits of such data, particularly selection effects along the criminal justice process and the risk of ecological inference.
Below this introductory text, two dropdown menus. The first allows users to select the category of criminal offences displayed. Each option corresponds to a distinct offence group defined in the Ministry of Justice statistical classification. For now there is only IPV. Selecting a different offence group dynamically updates the indicators and the map while preserving a common methodological framework, enabling systematic comparison across different types of criminal litigation.

The second drowning down menu is the one there is already in place.

On the right-hand side, a large interactive map of metropolitan France displays the results at the departmental level. Each department is color-coded according to a synthetic indicator of sentencing severity. The color gradient reflects the department’s relative position within the national distribution, making it possible to immediately identify geographic patterns, regional contrasts, and large-scale spatial gradients in criminal punishment.
Data Sources and Indicators
The judicial data displayed on the page come from the criminal litigation mapping produced by the Statistical Service of the Ministry of Justice, based on the Cassiopée information system. The data cover convictions handed down by first-instance criminal courts for adult defendants and are aggregated by department.

The severity indicator combines two central dimensions of punishment: the use of immediate custodial sentences and the average length of prison terms when incarceration is imposed. Where relevant, the indicator corrects for legally expected structural factors, such as recidivism, in order to better capture residual territorial differences in sentencing practices. The page is designed to highlight robust spatial regularities rather than to provide causal explanations at the level of individual decisions.
